Each side is its own copy of the app, drawn at 50%, with its own settings (also under its gear) and open item. A combination changes only Layout and Open items as, a style only the Style; a preset sets everything.

Layout, style, theme, font, width, density, comment style, accent, tags and images are the app's own settings, under the gear in its header and in the reader bar. Presets, Shuffle and loading act on the left (or only) app. Keys: T panel · j k move · Enter open · m read · / search · ← → previous/next and Esc back when an item covers the grid
