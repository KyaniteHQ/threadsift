# Brand direction

The brand is Quarto, a technical monograph: one grotesque family, a ruled matrix of hairlines, and a green ink. [The design system](../../DESIGN.md), Brand Brief, holds its values, and [the product brief](../../PRODUCT.md), Brand Brief, holds the idea, the signature element and the voice.

## Why Quarto

- **Its accent is an existing Accent.** Quarto's default accent is Moss, one of the five hues the Accent setting offers, so the brand needs no new hue.
- **The Font setting keeps 4 choices.** Quarto reads in a grotesque, so each of the 3 Styles writes a pair of its own: Schibsted Grotesk for Library, Schibsted Grotesk with Source Serif 4 for Broadsheet, and Commit Mono for Workbench. Atkinson Hyperlegible is the fourth. A brand that read in a serif already would leave Broadsheet no reading face of its own, and the Font setting would hold 3.

## The kept strip in the Reader

In a List row and a Grid card the kept strip is the first line of the cell. In the Reader the header opens with the title, and the strip sits under it, at the start of the line with the author and the date. The header then has no label above its heading, which the `impeccable` skill bans outright, so the design check needs no exception for the strip.

## Covers

A cover holds the Item's own first lines, the Topic tint and the Source mark, and no strip of its own. The kept strip directly above the cover in a Grid card already names the community or the Source, the handle, the language or the domain, and counts a thread's comments, so a strip on the cover would repeat it. A repository's stars and an Article's reading time, which the kept strip does not hold, go on the card's line under the title.
